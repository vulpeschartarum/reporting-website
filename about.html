Practical Reporting for Business Operations

Ledger Crow helps businesses turn operational data into useful reporting, dashboards, and recurring decision-making tools.

The focus is not on building flashy analytics projects. The focus is helping managers, service departments, operations teams, and business owners answer practical questions about performance, productivity, utilization, revenue, and workflow efficiency.

Experience includes SQL reporting, Crystal Reports development, Power BI dashboards, reporting automation, recurring report delivery, and business data cleanup. Much of this work comes from operational environments where reporting is tied directly to day-to-day decisions rather than executive presentations.

Typical projects include service department reporting, technician utilization tracking, work order analysis, KPI dashboards, recurring management reports, exception reporting, and replacing spreadsheet-driven processes with more reliable reporting workflows.

The goal is simple: help businesses spend less time gathering information and more time acting on it.
